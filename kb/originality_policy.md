# Originality Review
SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Every submission is compared with other teams' submissions. When the similarity score is strictly greater than 50 percent, the submission is flagged for human review by an authorised reviewer. A similarity score is an indicator only and is never by itself a reason for automatic rejection.

# Review Outcomes
A reviewer may clear the submission, request corrections, or reject it only under an approved rejection policy. Every decision records the reviewer, the reason and the evidence.
