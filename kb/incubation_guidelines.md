# Phase 1 Overview
SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Phase 1 of the incubation programme collects idea abstracts from student teams. Each team selects one domain and one problem statement from the approved catalogue.

# Team Formation
A team must have between two and three members. Team membership is locked immediately after registration and cannot be changed. Each member may belong to only one team.

# Domain and Problem Statement Selection
After registration the team selects a domain and a problem statement. The selection is locked once confirmed and cannot be changed later.

# Abstract Template
The abstract must contain these headings: Problem Statement / Motivation, Impact, Methodology, and Deliverables / Outcomes. Every section must contain real content (at least 15 words). The abstract must be written in English and contain at least 150 words and no more than 5000 words. Accepted file formats are PDF, DOCX and PPTX, with a maximum size of 10 MB.
